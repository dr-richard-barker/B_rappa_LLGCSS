The Provocation: "Perhaps It's Just a Classification Exercise?"

"Can machine learning latent models classify radiation stress across species? And does the biology stop at classification?"

Our investigation reveals a fundamental two-tiered reality:

  • At the Diagnostic Layer (Classification): YES. Treating spaceflight and radiation stress as an automated classification problem succeeds convincingly. The DeepSpace Stress-Recognition System (DSRS) queries 123 single-cell developmental seed panels and correctly top-ranks radiation_GCR (mean ρ = 0.1504) and acute gamma (rad_100Gy_co60, ρ = 0.2545, P < 10⁻⁴) across all 10 stress families. The 41D CVAE ROS Decoder accurately infers the post-exposure kinetic window as 3.84 hours, placing the samples squarely into the acute response nadir.
  • At the Biological Layer (Mechanistic Systems Biology): NO. The latent projections uncover rich, actionable biological discovery that classification models alone obscure:
    1. The "Antioxidant Shield Deficit": The absence of anthocyanin pigments in the anl mutant alters the chemical fate of ionizing radiation: WT plants buffer radiation ionization into a secondary hydrogen peroxide signaling cascade (51.1% probability), whereas anl mutants experience an unbuffered primary superoxide free-radical burst (24.9% probability) peaking at 2.23 hours.
    2. Constitutive Resilience Collapse: Sham-irradiated anl mutants already display depressed resilience (RRI = 0.638, housekeeping co-expression r = 0.079), identical to an acute 40 cGy GCR strike in WT plants.
    3. DNA Damage Response & Flavonoid Gateway: WT plants mount a robust induction of core double-strand-break repair (BRCA1 +2.03, PARP2 +0.86, RAD51 +0.16) and the upstream flavonoid shunt (PAL1 +0.61, CHS +0.77, F3H +1.51), while anl mutants exhibit a blunted, negative interaction response.

Experimental Design: 39 Factorial Libraries

The study utilizes 39 high-throughput RNA-seq libraries from the NASA Artemis Lunar LEAF candidate investigation in Wisconsin Fast Plants (Brassica rapa), structured as a full 3-way factorial design:

  • Factor 1: Radiation Dose — 0 cGy (Sham Control) vs. 40 cGy Galactic Cosmic Ray (GCR) simulation at Brookhaven National Laboratory (NSRL).
  • Factor 2: Genotype — Wild-Type (WT, anthocyanin-rich, dark purple stems) vs. anthocyaninless (anl, mutant lacking protective flavonoid pigments).
  • Factor 3: Spaceflight Preservative — Deep Space Preservative (DRS, ambient dry-storage stabilization hardware) vs. RNAlater (RL, laboratory wet standard).

Key Findings Summary

  1. Conserved GCR Transcriptional Programs: Cross-species single-cell projection through DSRS establishes that the molecular signature of 40 cGy GCR is deeply conserved between Arabidopsis and Brassica rapa.
  2. Preservative Fixative Parity (DRS vs RL): Deep Space Preservative exhibits exceptional fidelity to RNAlater across all 18,770 orthologs (Pearson r = 0.297, P < 10⁻¹⁵) and maintains RRI delta trajectory parity (0.706 → 0.655 in DRS vs 0.693 → 0.616 in RL; difference < 0.03), validating DRS hardware for autonomous lunar return missions.
  3. Cellular Meristematic Vulnerability: Spatial mapping via ggPlantMap indicates that root apical meristems (13.8%) and central vascular steles (12.5%) are primary damage foci, with floral reproductive whorls contributing >25% of total predicted stress susceptibility.
